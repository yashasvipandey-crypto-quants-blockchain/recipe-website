White Sauce Pasta

Preparation Time: 20 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • Pasta
  • Milk
  • Butter
  • Flour
  • Vegetables
  • Salt & Pepper

Instructions

  1. Boil pasta until soft.
  2. Prepare white sauce using butter, flour, and milk.
  3. Add vegetables and cook for 5 minutes.
  4. Mix pasta with sauce.
  5. Serve hot.
