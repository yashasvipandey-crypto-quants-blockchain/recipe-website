Paneer Butter Masala

⏱ Time: 35 mins

🔥 Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

  • Paneer cubes
  • Tomato puree
  • Butter
  • Cream
  • Spices
  • Onion

Instructions

  1. Heat butter and sauté onions.
  2. Add tomato puree and spices.
  3. Cook until oil separates.
  4. Add paneer and cream.
  5. Simmer for 10 minutes and serve.
