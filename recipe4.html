Vegetable Fried Rice

Preparation Time: 25 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Nutrition (Approx)

Calories: 250 kcal

Protein: 6g

Carbs: 40g

Ingredients

  • Cooked rice
  • Mixed vegetables
  • Soy sauce
  • Garlic
  • Oil
  • Salt & Pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat oil and sauté garlic.
  2. Add vegetables and cook for 5 minutes.
  3. Add rice and mix well.
  4. Add soy sauce and seasoning.
  5. Cook for 3-4 minutes and serve.
