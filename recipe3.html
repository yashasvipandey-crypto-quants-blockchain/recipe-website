Chocolate Cake

Preparation Time: 50 minutes

Difficulty: Hard

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Sugar
  • Cocoa powder
  • Eggs
  • Butter
  • Baking powder

Instructions

1. Mix all dry ingredients.
2. Add eggs and butter.
3. Pour into baking tray.
4. Bake at 180°C for 30 mins.
5. Let it cool and serve.
💡 Tip: Add a little coffee to enhance chocolate flavor!
