Veg Sandwich

Preparation Time: 10 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Quick Budget Friendly Healthy

Ingredients

  • Bread slices
  • Butter
  • Tomato
  • Cucumber
  • Cheese
  • Salt & Pepper

Instructions

  1. Spread butter on bread slices.
  2. Add vegetables evenly.
  3. Sprinkle salt and pepper.
  4. Add cheese slice.
  5. Cover and cut into halves. Serve fresh.
